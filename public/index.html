About Me

A Product person who builds.

A Product Manager who builds, with two years owning product workstreams from problem definition through to delivery. I came up through every level of a business, from frontline and specialist roles into business analysis and product, so I know how a ticket or a workflow actually lands on the person using it.

That range lets me translate fluently between what a business wants and what an engineer can build.

I do not just specify tools, I make them. I code in React, JavaScript, and TypeScript, and I work AI-natively with Claude to draft requirements, prototype flows, and ship real projects, several of which are below.

When not building, I can usually be found at board game events, organising them or teaching newcomers, and I have a standing interest in UX and system design, usually alongside a science podcast.
